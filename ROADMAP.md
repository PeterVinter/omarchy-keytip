# Roadmap

Ideas, not promises.

- Settings in `shell.json` instead of constants in `Service.qml` (timings, corner, cooldown).
- Choose the corner the card appears in.
- Type your own combination instead of picking a suggestion.
- Show non-launch shortcuts that are useful for the app that opened.
- Undo for the last added shortcut, from the confirmation card.
- More languages.
